# Museum Buddy

bad problem: In general, when I revisit and reread things, I wish I knew what I was thinking before. But when I first did them, I didn't because the friction to jot it down and store it was too much. I would need to find somewhere to write it down, store it, and years down the line find it again.
- provides organization

solution:

- take picture of label -> with label and location data, software figures out what art it is -> record thoughts about art (text or voice to transcribe)
- background: store additional context, like time of year, time of day, month, weather
- aggregate thoughts and dates of thoughts by art
- share thoughts on a particular date or all dates of art
- if there has not been an exact same experience, find a similar one (same time period, theme, artist)
- maybe expand to books or other things you can watch / consume

- dynamic and collaborative aspect
- evidence to support bad situation exists - affects other people beyond you (how would users collaborate)
- blog posts, articles, decision fatigue - can be impactful for a subset of users (no need for scale)
- practice of reflection
- classroom dynamic
- only see posts after you post one yourself

# Room Heater

bad problem: I want to save on AC / heating bills. How do I arrange my bed and furniture so I am in a spot that's naturally cooler or warmer? When should I open and close my blinds and windows?

solution:
- input room layout (grid where they can drag things), fixed things such as windows, where radiators / acs are, direction of window (cardinal)
- input temp preferences at each location and where the user is
- suggest best arrangement for bed and furniture
- suggest when to open and close blinds

dynamics and collaborative
- store some state and evolve over time
- different users, want actions and behaviors to not be independent
- collaborative - roommates, organizing a common space
- justify need for app - corroboration
